Drawing JSON
Pick a drawing, then drag annotations or views to move them; Tab cycles what can be selected (annotations, views, geometry). Aligned views slide only along their projection axis and follow their parent view. Edit the JSON and press Re-render (R) to update. Shift-drag a dimension to move only its line (text stays in place along it). A selected datum, or a leader ending in a dot, has a handle on its end at the part: drag it along the edge (past it onto an extension line) or across the face; a selected ordinate label has one on its leader's end nearest the view, to slide that end along the leader. Double-click resets one item, right-click an annotation to edit its JSON, or a view for arrows that add a projected view on that side and a Section view switch; in Views mode drag a section's arrows to move its cutting plane, or a detail's circle to move it (right-click a detail for its size and shape); drag a view's label (SECTION A-A) to move it relative to its view (click the title block for its JSON and the sheet size), in its JSON window Merge with… puts a thread, note, frame or dimension on the same feature onto its leader (click one, or Ctrl+click several), Del deletes the selected one (Ctrl+Z brings it back). In Geometry mode click faces, edges and points to get their selector (Ctrl+click adds). Arrow keys nudge the selection (Shift ×10). Drag the background to pan, scroll to zoom.
