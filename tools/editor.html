Drawing editor

Choose a drawing JSONfrom the list, open one, or drop it here. stepdraw renders it, then dimensions, GD&T frames, datums and notes can be dragged; their leaders and extension lines stay attached.

Pick a drawing, then drag annotations or views to move them; Tab cycles what can be selected (annotations, views, geometry). Aligned views slide only along their projection axis and follow their parent view. Edit the JSON and press Re-render (R) to update. Shift-drag a dimension to move only its line (text stays in place along it). Double-click resets one item, right-click an annotation or a view to edit its JSON (click the title block for it and the sheet size), Del deletes the selected one (Ctrl+Z brings it back). In Geometry mode click faces, edges and points to get their selector (Ctrl+click adds). Arrow keys nudge the selection (Shift ×10). Drag the background to pan, scroll to zoom.
